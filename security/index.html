01Protecting the research process

MacBrains Labs studies Apple Silicon inference and maintains the software supporting that work. These project-owner advisories document two fixed defects in its private research automation tooling: unsafe archive destination construction and inconsistent validation of derivative research records.

Both arose in one June 2026 hardening effort. Later validation compared the vulnerable source, the repair commit and a pinned later revision in isolated copies. The work was operator-directed by Nayte Wilson and AI-assisted.

03Public research maintenance

Nayte Wilson also maintains ANE Research Records, a separate public repository of source-pinned measurement summaries and verification tools. Its security policy provides a private reporting contact.

The publication audit checks public release archives against the source tag and tests the handling of corrupted or inconsistent inputs. This verifies archive integrity; it does not independently reproduce private ANE measurements. The two advisories above concern different, private tooling.

04Corrections and security reports

Send a suspected defect, exposed information or evidence inconsistency to research@macbrainslabs.org. Include the affected page or repository, revision if known, observed behavior and a minimal description. Keep credentials, personal data and sensitive reproduction details out of public issues.

The public contact is also available through security.txt. Publication does not grant permission to test third-party systems. Research scope and authorization are described in the methods statement.
