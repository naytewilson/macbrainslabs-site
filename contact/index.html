Research scope and independence

MacBrains Labs is an independent research identity used for hands-on engineering work. It is not presented here as a separately incorporated company, a university department, an Apple partner or a licensed laboratory. Public technical results describe experiments conducted on owned systems and authorized software artifacts, not endorsement or certification by Apple or model developers.

We investigate graph construction and inference backends, MLComputePlan placement, native Apple Silicon measurement, model quantization, reproducible benchmark methods, runtime reliability and authorized defensive assessment of software under our control.

Requests for technical evidence

When contacting us about a result, please identify the model, graph or function, the claimed measurement, your reproduction environment and any inconsistent observation. The public site summarizes selected records; not every underlying source artifact or model file is publicly distributable.

SECURITY AND TESTING BOUNDARY

MacBrains Labs does not authorize testing of third-party systems by publishing this site. Any defensive security work or vulnerability analysis requires authorization from the system owner and remains bounded to that authorization.

Operating context

United States · Maine. Research is conducted using Apple Silicon development hardware and associated authorized compute infrastructure. Physical and legal contact details can be furnished to appropriate verified counterparties through private channels when required; they are not listed on the public website.
