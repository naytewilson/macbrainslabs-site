The practice

Work at the graph boundary.

“Runs on ANE” is not a binary claim. Different functions, shapes and output tails may be assigned to different devices. We separate compiler placement from numeric correctness and measured throughput.

  1. 01

    Hand-authored MIL

    Generate MILSpec programs from native Swift, assemble ML packages and compile with Apple's public Core ML toolchain. No entitlement bypass or private kernel access.

    The build path
  2. 02

    Account for every op

    Inspect MLComputePlan assignments for state access, slices, projections, norms and output tails. A declared ANE preference is not proof of ANE residency.

    The ANE audit
  3. 03

    Parity, or it fails

    Compare sealed tensor fixtures with CPU and ANE outputs: cosine similarity, relative L2, max absolute error, and finite-value gates at each exposed boundary.

    Parity case study
  4. 04

    Falsify the ceiling

    Measure actual batch and sequence shapes. A token/s figure is discarded if its underlying model was shape-locked to one token per call.

    Decode regimes

Interpretation matters

A model with attention and KV machinery assigned to ANE may still execute a CPU argmax tail. We report both numbers instead of rounding the entire inference path up to “100% ANE.”

Research scope

Independent. Instrumented. Reproducible.

MacBrains Labs is an independent engineering research identity for Apple Silicon and on-device inference. The work emphasizes native runtimes, quantized transformer execution, compiler behavior, and authorized analysis of software and infrastructure under our control.

We do not claim Apple affiliation, privileged ANE access, or universal placement results. Individual measurements refer to the named artifact, backend, tensor shape and hardware configuration. Private source artifacts are not implicitly public merely because a summary appears here.
